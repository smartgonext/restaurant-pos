 The Perfect Selective Cash Drawer Solution
To make the cash drawer open ONLY when you click "Cash" (and remain closed during UPI, GPay, or Card payments), follow these simple steps:

Step 1: Disable the Automatic Windows Driver Kick
We need to tell Windows to stop opening the drawer automatically on every print document:

Open your Windows Settings ➔ Devices ➔ Printers & Scanners.
Select your Thermal Receipt Printer (e.g., POS-80 or XP-80) and click Manage ➔ Printer Properties.
Go to the Device Settings tab (or Advanced Settings / Peripheral settings depending on your driver).
Locate the setting labeled Cash Drawer or Peripheral Unit Type or Drawer Kick-out.
Set it to "None" or "Disabled" (instead of "Open Before Printing" or "Open After Printing").
Click Apply and OK.
Now, regular print jobs will print receipts silently without kicking the drawer!

Step 2: Let our Software take 100% Selective Control
Our newly implemented direct-spooling software is already perfectly engineered for this! Since we bypassed standard printer drivers and built a direct raw channel, the system will behave exactly as you requested:

UPI / GPay / Card	Prints the receipt beautifully	🔒 Stays locked closed (no signal sent)
Cash Checkout	Prints the receipt beautifully	🔓 Instantly kicks open (software sends raw signal)
